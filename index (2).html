Our Story

Serving Islamabad since 1993.

Delight Pharmacy has been a trusted name in Islamabad for over three decades, opening its doors in 1993 with a simple promise: genuine medicine and honest advice, every single time. What began as a single counter in Blue Area has grown into a pharmacy that generations of families rely on.

Today we operate two branches across the city — our original Blue Area store and a second location opposite PIMS Hospital — so that quality care and fully stocked shelves are never far away. Every prescription is still checked personally by our pharmacist, the same way it was in 1993.
